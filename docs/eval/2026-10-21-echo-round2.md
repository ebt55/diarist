# Eval: echo round two, the layered gate (2026-10-21)

Plan 15 section 9 and the external review's changes in section 9.1 (`docs/plans/15-echo-without-headphones.md`); the facts behind them are in `docs/research/15-echo-status-for-external-review.md` sections 5 and 6 and `docs/research/16-echo-approaches.md`. Release build, AMD Ryzen 9 5900X, RTX 3080 for the transcriptions (EN model, lookahead 13, `v3-offline`). No transcript text is reproduced here.

What shipped (`crates/core/src/audio/echo/layers.rs`, one decision for the live view, the live run at Stop and the post-pass):

- **Text**: a mic word matches a loopback token of the same normalised text whose start lies −0.15 to +1.0 s before it; within any 4 consecutive mic words spanning at most 2 s, 3 or more matches with lags within 0.3 s of each other (0.5 s since the transfer gate, below) are echo; 2, when one is a content word of 5 or more letters, only with the tie-breaker (coefficient at the keep bound on a correlated recording, else level within 6 dB of the floor); single words never.
- **Level**: the 90th percentile of 20 ms speech-band (300 to 3,400 Hz) frames over `[start − 300 ms, end + 80 ms]` within 6 dB of the floor (the 20th percentile of the mic frames whose loopback frame is silent) while the far end is active, and not within 6 dB of the owner's level (the median level of the unmatched words spoken while the far end was silent). Not after the canceller.
- **Correlation**: the round-one statistic on a 300 to 3,400 Hz band-pass (also the monitor's), look-back 350 ms; raw band 0.30 / 0.50 / 0.70, self-calibrated per recording from 20 text-matched words (drop bound within 0.55 to 0.80); after the canceller 0.60 / 0.70 / 0.95, fixed.
- **Utterance rule**: in an engine utterance (live) or a stretch with pauses up to 300 ms (post-pass) whose words are at least 60 percent flagged, unflagged pockets of up to 2 words within 300 ms of a flagged word, far end active, not near the owner's level.
- **Engagement**: the correlation where the recording correlates (coupled, a canceller ran, `always`); the other layers also once the text matches show echo (8 or more run words, at least 1 percent of the mic words). Nothing else runs, so a headphone recording without a leak is left as it was.
- **Attenuation** (`10·log10(E_mic / E_mic-aec)` per word, review item 4): measured, not engaged (below).
- **Transfer** (added in the afternoon, section "Transfer gate" below): after the post-pass canceller, the decision on the raw mic's words carried over to the cancelled words the canceller cut.
- Live: the loopback's revisions reach the gate; a mic utterance's decision can change for 3 s after its latest revision, then it is final; Stop decides every word at once.

## Transfer gate (plan 15 section 9.2, 21 Oct 2026, afternoon)

Meeting 16 (speakers, 7.5 min, loud echo) left 21 duplicates in the 293 words the post-pass kept (7.17 percent), against 0.43 percent live: after the canceller the residue comes out of the speech model as garbled fragments the post-canceller layers miss, while the raw mic, which the model heard cleanly, still repeats the far end in clean runs. What shipped (`crates/core/src/audio/echo/transfer.rs`, `crates/core/src/live/postpass.rs`):

- **Raw source**: when the canceller ran, the live run's mic words (every word of each mic utterance's latest revision in `live_utterances`, already on meeting time), else the file engine once more over `mic.wav` (undiarized, the file settings). Crash recovery and re-runs go through the same post-pass (the live run is kept until it is done); a live run without mic words falls back to the transcription.
- **Raw gate**: the layered gate on those words with all layers and the raw band (0.30 / 0.50 / 0.70, calibrated as live), levels and correlation on the raw mic, text against the post-pass's loopback words.
- **Transfer**: a cancelled word whose span widened by 0.3 s each side overlaps a flagged raw word and no unflagged one is a candidate; it goes when the guard lets it (below); a cancelled word with no raw word near it stays. Reason `transfer` in `echo_dropped`; `engine_info.echo.transfer` has the source, the raw gate's counts and band, the counts per guard and the wall times.
- **Post-canceller layers**: still run on the cancelled words as shipped (band 0.60 / 0.70 / 0.95, utterance rule), the text layer with a 500 ms lag spread (item 2 below); a word goes when either removes it.

Guards (the brief's): (a) **text**: the cancelled word's normalised text equals, or is one edit from, an overlapping flagged raw word's; (b) **level**: the canceller cut it by 3 dB or more over `[start − 300 ms, end + 80 ms]` and its level after the canceller sits 12 dB or more under the owner's level (the layers' definition, on the cancelled words); **either**: (a) or (b); **plain**: no guard.
