# Eval: echo monitor and word gate (2026-10-20)

Plan 15, chunk A (`docs/plans/15-echo-without-headphones.md`). Release build, AMD Ryzen 9 5900X, RTX 3080 for the fixture's transcription. Gate at the defaults: span [start − 350 ms, end + 160 ms], lag tracked per 2 s block ±20 ms, FFT correlation at sample resolution, hysteresis drop ≥ 0.5 / keep < 0.2 / neighbours at `echo.gate_threshold` 0.35. No transcript text is reproduced here.

## Meeting 12 (111.6 minutes, meeting on speakers)

`crates/core/tests/echo_sample.rs` over the stored `playback.flac` and the runtime's word files, on meeting time through the clock anchors (a copy of the database). Classes are the measurement's labels (plan 15 section 2): a duplicate is a mic word with the same text as a loopback word within ±0.8 s or inside a matched 3-word run; the owner's words are non-duplicates whose ±5 neighbours are at most 30 percent duplicates; misrecognised echo is the rest. "During far-end speech": a 20 ms loopback frame over −50 dBFS inside the word.

| Class | Words | Result | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duplicates dropped | 12,260 | 99.37 % | ≥ 99 % |
| Misrecognised echo dropped | 905 | 86.52 % | ≥ 90 % (missed) |
| of them during far-end speech | 748 | 96.52 % | |
| Owner's words kept | 1,462 | 98.50 % | ≥ 98 % |
| Owner's words kept during far-end speech | 172 | 94.77 % | ≥ 93 % |
| Mic words after the gate | 14,627 before | 1,639 | |
| Duplicates (labels) among kept words | | 4.70 % | |

Misrecognised echo misses its bar: of the 122 such words the gate keeps, 96 have no loopback frame over −50 dBFS anywhere in their span and their coefficient is low (median 0.07, p90 0.18), so at signal level they are not echo; the label comes from their neighbours. Tried: threshold 0.25 (87.18 %, owner 98.08 %), span 500/200 ms (87.85 %, owner 98.08 %, during far-end 95.93 %). Raw channel indices without clock maps give the same table; the tracked lag follows the mic clock's drift (3.10 ms per minute, 346 ms at the end).

Detector: coupled at 20 s (the tenth active block), never released; coupling 0.72 (median of 2,453 active blocks), lag 3.25 ms on meeting time. With the mic shifted 7 s (null) it stays clear over the whole recording (median 0.036). Return loss by the plan's formula (with the NCC term): 13.1 dB offline, 14.2 dB from the replayed live monitor (the WAVs carry no AGC gains); the measured level difference was 10.3 dB, the NCC term adds about 2.9 dB because the room spreads the echo beyond one lag.

## FLEURS double-talk fixture

`crates/core/tests/echo_fleurs.rs`: the 55 FLEURS English utterances of `fleurs_fetch.py --langs en` (1,182 reference words) placed at random times, 1 to 13 s apart, as the owner at −33 dBFS over 20 minutes of meeting 12's far end (from minute 1), with the far end into the mic 3 ms later and 10 dB down (no room response) and a −59 dBFS floor; 40 of the 55 utterances have more than 30 percent of their frames over far-end speech. The real post-pass (EN model, lookahead 13, `v3-offline`) runs with `echo.mode` off and auto. "Me" WER per utterance over the words whose middle falls in its span (−0.2 s, +0.4 s); echo words are "me" words outside every span.

| `echo.gate_threshold` | "me" WER, off → on | over far-end speech | in far-end pauses | Echo words removed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.35 (default) | 29.61 → 45.52 % | 36.71 → 59.29 % | 11.45 → 10.24 % | 99.56 % (1,135 → 5) |
| 0.45 | 29.61 → 34.26 % | 36.71 → 43.65 % | 11.45 → 10.24 % | 99.56 % |
| 0.5 | 29.61 → 31.73 % | 36.71 → 40.12 % | 11.45 → 10.24 % | 99.56 % |
| 0.6 | 29.61 → 30.29 % | 36.71 → 38.24 % | 11.45 → 9.94 % | 99.56 % |

The target (at most +1 point with at least 99 percent of echo words removed) is met at 0.6 and missed at the default. Spoken over continuous far-end speech, an owner word's coefficient is the echo's share of the mic, about 0.45 to 0.5 when the owner is 5 dB over the echo, the same as an echo word recognised from the mixture, so the middle band cannot tell them apart; only cancellation can. The same thresholds on meeting 12:

| `echo.gate_threshold` | Duplicates dropped | Misrecognised echo | Owner kept | Owner kept during far-end speech |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.35 | 99.37 % | 86.52 % | 98.50 % | 94.77 % |
| 0.45 | 99.25 % | 85.86 % | 98.70 % | 96.51 % |
| 0.5 | 99.13 % | 85.30 % | 98.77 % | 96.51 % |
| 0.6 | 98.62 % | 84.20 % | 98.77 % | 96.51 % |

No threshold in the accepted range meets both sets of bars: 0.6 passes the fixture and drops duplicates to 98.62 percent; 0.5 keeps every meeting 12 bar but misrecognised echo and costs 2.1 points on the fixture. The fixture's double-talk is far denser than meeting 12's (11.8 percent of the owner's words overlap the far end there).
