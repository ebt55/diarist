# Eval: the Nemotron 3.5 re-time owed from beta.5 (31 Oct 2026)

Plan 18 chunk U, item 1 (`docs/plans/18-beta6.md` section 10.1), closing plan 17 section 10's re-time item: the beta.5 release check timed Nemotron 3.5 at −5.4 percent against beta.4 on the gate set, the shortfall sitting in three runs (Aug 9, Sep Logistics, Aug 14) that overlapped the Claude desktop app's GPU use (docs/eval/2026-10-27-beta5-release.md, "Speed, gate set"). The owner accepted the speed bar on 27 Oct with a re-time owed in beta.6. This note is that re-time: the same three sessions on runtime 0.2.0, Nemotron 3.5, on an idle machine. Opus 5.5 measurement agent, inside chunk R's timed window.

## The bar (plan 18 section 10.1, fixed before the run)

"Pooled seconds over the three sessions within 5 percent of beta.4's 32.4 + 32.7 + 54.7 = 119.8 s (at most 125.8 s), with a clean pmon log (no process but nemo-speech above 5 percent SM in any sample)." The orchestrator's brief for this run restated it with claude.exe excepted, as the beta.5 rule does, and asked for claude.exe's peak SM per run; the runs below are clean under both readings.

**Verdict: the bar passes. Pooled 118.6 s against a bar of 125.8 s (beta.4 119.8 s), 1.0 percent faster than beta.4, with a clean pmon log under both readings (claude.exe at 0 percent SM in every sample).** The beta.5 block's 131.8 s for these sessions was the environment, as that note supposed: the same executable, models and command line take 118.6 s on an idle machine.
