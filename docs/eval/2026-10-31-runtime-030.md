# Eval: runtime 0.3.0 against 0.2.0, the gate of chunk R (31 Oct 2026)

Plan 18 chunk R (`docs/plans/18-beta6.md` section 4, with the 31 Oct addendum 4.4): does Diarist move from nemo-speech v0.2.0 to the tagged v0.3.0? Both ASR models, Nemotron EN (the default) and Nemotron 3.5, on the owner's Zoom recordings plus AMI ES2002a and on the public meeting groups, with the live path, the package and our tests. Opus 5.5 measurement agent. Numbers are percentages unless a column says otherwise.

The "Rules" section below was written and committed before any run on 0.3.0, with every v0.2.0 reference number rescored by today's `tools/eval/metrics.py`. No 0.3.0 number had been produced or seen when it was committed. Nothing in it changes after a 0.3.0 number is seen; a miss by any margin is a miss, and the note records the margin (plan 18 section 13).

### Method details fixed before any 0.3.0 run

The plan leaves these open; they are fixed here and not changed after.

- **The broad set** is the 29 sessions of the gate set and the broad group together (Aug 2, Aug 9, Sep 7, Sep 5, Sep Logistics, Aug 14, AMI ES2002a; Aug 1, 3 to 8, 10 to 13, 15; Sep 1 to 4, 6; Jul 1 to 5), 1,340.9 min: the only reading under which both of the plan's figures (29 sessions, 1,341 min) hold. The talks (folder D) are not in it. Its seven gate sessions are the gate set's unmodified 0.3.0 runs, not run twice; for them R4's broad-set escalation is the gate rule itself, already run.
- **Both sides run the same pipeline.** The app's file pass (`--stream --endpointing --diarize`, lookahead 13 and `v3-offline` from the manifest, no `engine_args`), loop collapse off and punctuation off, `--no-diar-pass`, as the v0.2.0 runs read here were made (research/19's `rs_*_shipped` by a 0.1.0-beta.4 build without the collapse; N2's `shipped_*_ph*`; the public baseline's `shipped_*`). New configs `shipped_en_030`, `shipped_en_030_ph70`, `_ph20`, `_ph40` and the same for 3.5 (`tools/eval/configs.toml`, appended after N2's), with `DIARIST_EVAL_ENGINE_DIR` at the extracted CUDA package. diarist-cli is N2's build from `00cc9a6`, SHA-256 `dc4c7ff307791359e557b84e07ef0600d0ebfc4c1b493e7b478c02364b635003`, the build that made N2's offset runs. Models: byte copies of N2's, SHA-256 equal to `manifests/models.json` (EN `d9a01898…3812d`, 3.5 `a5c435f2…429ae`, diarizer `08456d9e…a3a1`).
- **Counts.** Deletions are `metrics.py`'s `w_del`, gaps its `gaps` and `gaps_5s`, cpWER its `cpwer`, all as N2 used them; pooled means summed over the sessions. New gaps of 5 s or more are `phase.py`'s `new_5s` against `rs_*_shipped`. The R4 ceilings are the plan's table, used as printed; a session passes when 0.3.0's four-run mean of `metrics.py`'s cpWER, rounded to two decimals, is at most its ceiling.
- **R4's broad-set trip** is 0.3.0's cpWER minus `rs_*_shipped`'s, both at two decimals, above 0.50. An escalated session runs at −70, +20 and +40 ms on both runtimes (N2's `shipped_*_ph*` configs for v0.2.0, the `*_030_ph*` configs for 0.3.0); its v0.2.0 range is the largest minus the smallest of its four v0.2.0 cpWERs.
- **R5's pooling** is `levers.py baseline`'s: pooled cpWER is the sum of `cp_errors` over the sum of `cp_length`, pooled WDER is weighted by `wder_pairs`. The reference is the rescored v0.2.0 value at two decimals (below); each rounds to the public baseline's printed figure, so the bar is the stricter of the two readings by at most 0.05.
- **R6.** `live_ws_gpu` per model with `DIARIST_LIVE_SESSIONS=2 DIARIST_LIVE_SECONDS=60 DIARIST_LIVE_RIGHT_CONTEXT=6`, as the live-lookahead note ran it; the latency bars on its pooled first-word figures; "two sessions under 2 GB" read as the server with both sessions (peak minus before) under 2,048 MiB. `live_carry_repro` on a copy of meeting 16 at `DIARIST_LIVE_RIGHT_CONTEXT=6`: the test's own assertion (every name given during the replay reaches a post-pass speaker), with its carry-over figures beside the live-lookahead note's R 6 column. The plan 15 echo replay is `echo_sample` over that replay's database, as the live-lookahead note's section (c) ran it; "within 0.5 points" is read against that note's R 6 column (residual duplicates 0.43 % post-pass and 0.00 % live; owner's words kept 91.94 % and 99.07 %), and a move of more than 0.5 points in the worse direction (more residual duplicates, fewer of the owner's words kept) fails; a larger move in the better direction is reported, as that note read it. The fixture is re-recorded with the app's serve flags at lookahead 6, EN model, 60 s of AMI ES2002a from 0 s as 600 frames of 100 ms, then a commit, as the 15 Oct fixture; the adapter's fixture test (`live_ws::tests::replays_the_recorded_session`, which rebuilds its expectations from the fixture) must pass on the new recording.
- **R7.** × real time is the gate set's audio over the `nemo-speech transcribe` step's wall time (`asr_wall_s`, model load included), pooled per model, from the unmodified 0.3.0 runs made with `--transcribe-only --no-diar-pass` under quiet.lock with `nvidia-smi pmon -s u -d 1` for the whole block, after a clean 60 s pmon check; a run is valid when no process other than nemo-speech and claude.exe is above 5 % SM in any sample of its window (claude.exe's peak is reported per run). The bar is the pooled figure over seven valid runs per model; an invalid run is re-timed in a later clean window, and if one cannot be, R7's speed is "not measured validly" and the verdict says R7 is pending, not failed. Peak VRAM, file pass: the largest `vram_asr_peak_delta_mib` over the gate set's 0.3.0 unmodified runs against the largest over `rs_*_shipped`'s gate runs, per model. Server: `live_ws_gpu`'s server-and-two-sessions peak against the live-lookahead note's R 6 figure per model.
- **R8.** `cargo fmt --all -- --check`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings` and `cargo test --workspace` on the adoption commits, and `nemo-speech --json doctor` on the extracted CUDA package: healthy as research/08 section 5 defines it (`driver_runtime_compatible`, `features.backend_cuda`, a `gpu` device) and `version` 0.3.0.
- **GPU.** Every job through the shared queue (`gpu-run2.sh`); only the timed block holds quiet.lock.
